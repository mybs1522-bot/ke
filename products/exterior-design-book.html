About Reviews
VOLUME 05 · TECHNICAL REFERENCE MANUAL

Exterior Elevations, Facade Proportions & Outdoor Living

Facade Symmetry, Roof Overhang Pitch, Entryway Canopies, Sun Shading & Patio Steps

A building's facade is its public identity. Inconsistent window header lines, disproportionate roof pitches, or cramped front porches immediately signal amateur architecture. This volume provides classical and contemporary proportion formulas, weather overhang depths, and outdoor living clearances.

Critical On-Site Errors This Manual Prevents

ERROR 01

Mismatched Window Header Planes

Windows installed at slightly differing heights across elevations creating visual discordance. Clear alignment rules for exterior lintels and soffit returns.

ERROR 02

Inadequate Rain Canopy Overhangs

Entry porches too shallow to protect guests from driving rain while unlocking doors. Mandatory 48" to 60" landing depths with integrated drip edge details.

ERROR 03

Awkward Exterior Step Ratios

Steep garden steps that cause tripping. Certified ergonomic formula of 7" maximum rise to 11" minimum tread depth for effortless outdoor transitions.

ERROR 04

Overheating From Zero Eaves Shading

Modern flat facades without overhangs causing unbearable summer heat gain. Solar azimuth charts to calculate precise soffit projection depths.

Core Blueprint Standards & Modules Included

MOD 01
Classical & Modern Facade Symmetry

Golden Ratio fenestration rhythm, solid-to-void massing balance, and horizontal datum plane hierarchy.

MOD 02
Covered Porch & Portico Proportions

Structural column spacing, beam depth ratios, and ceiling height formulas relative to main facade scale.

MOD 03
Outdoor Kitchen & BBQ Clearances

Non-combustible wall clearances, stainless counter depths (30"), ventilation hoods, and gas line safety shutoff zones.

MOD 04
Patio Dining & Lounge Circulation

36" chair pullback envelopes, fire pit safety perimeters (48" from seating), and hardscape slope drainage.
